Write these 9 cards for this destination. Each card is a separate field of the JSON answer.

quickFacts ("Quick Facts"): items in this order, leaving out any that do not apply or you are unsure of:
Country (omit if the destination is a country) · Capital or main city · Official languages · Currency (name and
ISO code) · Time zone (UTC offset; note daylight saving if used) · Population (rounded, starting with "about";
countries and cities only) · Known for (3-5 things, comma-separated).

visa ("Visa & Entry Rules"): general entry rules for visiting the destination's country as a tourist. Cover, where
they exist: membership of a shared visa area (for example Schengen) · electronic visa or travel authorisation
systems by official name (for example ETA, eVisa, ESTA) · visa on arrival · passport validity commonly required ·
well-known entry requirements (for example onward ticket, yellow fever certificate). Only list exempt nationalities
when it is a broad, well-known rule (for example "EU citizens need no visa"). The last item: label "Check before
you go"; detail naming the official authority; its official website in "link" only if certain.

seasons ("Best Time to Visit"): "Peak season" (months and why) · "Off-peak season" (months and what to expect) ·
"Shoulder season" (if meaningful) · "Weather" (climate by season; typical temperature ranges in °C may be given) ·
"Big events" (1-3 recurring festivals or events with their usual month or dates; mayChange true) · "Best time to
visit" (one clear recommendation).

attractions ("Top Attractions"): 5-6 of the most famous real landmarks, sights or experiences in or very near the
destination. label = the name as commonly known; detail = what it is and why visit. Typical opening hours or entry
fees may be added (mayChange true). The attraction's official website in "link" only if certain.

hotels ("Best Areas to Stay"): 3-5 of the best areas, neighbourhoods or streets to book a hotel or resort, each with
who it suits (for example first-time visitors, budget, luxury, families, nightlife) and why. Optionally one item on
a type of stay special to the destination (for example ryokan, riad, houseboat). Typical nightly price ranges may be
added (mayChange true). Do not recommend individual hotels.

cuisine ("Food & Dining"): 4-5 characteristic local dishes or food experiences (label = dish name; detail = what it
is), then 1 item labelled "Dining tip" on local eating customs or etiquette. Famous long-standing food streets or
markets may be named (mayChange true).

transport ("Getting Around"): "From the airport" (the main airport by name and the usual ways into town; typical
fares may be given, mayChange true) · 2-4 items for the main ways to get around (for example metro, buses, trams,
taxis, widely used ride-hailing apps by name, ferries, trains between cities) · 1 "Tip" item.

insurance ("Travel Insurance"): "Is it required?" (only say required if it is an established entry requirement, for
example for a Schengen visa; otherwise "not required but recommended") · "Medical care" (quality and cost level:
low, moderate or high) · 1-3 risks specific to this destination that insurance should cover (for example altitude,
monsoon, adventure sports, theft) · "What to check" (one practical tip).

currency ("Money & Connectivity"): "Currency" (name and ISO code) · "Exchange rate" (approximate rate against the
traveller's currency, or the US dollar if it is the same as the local one, mayChange true) · "Cards or cash" (how widely cards and mobile payments are accepted) · "Tipping" (local
custom) · "Mobile data" (tourist SIM or eSIM availability; main networks may be named, mayChange true) · "Power
plugs" (plug type letters and voltage) · "Emergency number" (national emergency number; only if certain).
